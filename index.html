LCD Lector Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

LCD Lector is a private tool used only by the owner of the Instagram account @la_ciencia_dice to read data about that account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the app accesses

Through the Instagram API, and only for the @la_ciencia_dice account, the app reads:

  • Basic profile information and published media.
  • Insights (statistics) about the account and its posts.
  • Comments on the account's posts, including the commenter's username and comment text as provided by Instagram.

The app does not post, comment, reply, send messages or modify anything on Instagram.

How the data is used

The data is used only to help the account owner review the performance of their own content and read what their audience says. It is not used for advertising or profiling.

Sharing

The data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties.

Storage and retention

Data is stored on a computer controlled by the account owner and kept only as long as needed for the purposes above. The access token granted by Instagram expires after 60 days unless renewed.

Data deletion

To request deletion of any data the app holds about you, email val3nfranc0@gmail.com with your Instagram username. The request will be handled within 30 days. The account owner can also revoke the app's access at any time from Instagram settings under Apps and websites.

Contact

Questions about this policy: val3nfranc0@gmail.com.
